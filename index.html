02 · Under the hoodB58 3.0-litre TwinPower Turbo inline-six

One twin-scroll turbo, direct injection, VALVETRONIC and Double-VANOS, plus a 48-volt mild-hybrid motor. On this facelifted X5 (2024 onward) the motor sits inside the 8-speed gearbox, where it adds a short boost when you pull away. Start it up and the hood opens on the 3D engine bay, which shakes at idle. Then tap Rev it or drag the slider to hear the inline-six and the turbo spool. The sound is synthesized, not a recording.

03 · LightingAdaptive full-LED headlights

From the 2025 model year, the xDrive40i comes with adaptive full-LED headlights as standard. They turn with the steering and switch high beams on and off automatically. BMW also sold matrix LED headlights with Selective Beam, a high beam that dims only the part aimed at oncoming cars, often bundled with dark M Shadowline trim. Try both modes below; your window sticker shows which set you have.

08 · WorkshopCam straps, not bungees

The X5's cargo area has lashing eyes. Anything you tie to them should hold in a hard stop. Drag the slider to see why a strap that can't stretch is the right choice.
